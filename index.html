01 About Me

10+ years of growing teams, partnerships, and hands-on science education.

I started my career in 2015 as a Junior Executive in sales at OnnoRokom Group. Over 10+ years I have grown with the same organization, from selling on the field to leading five core functions as Assistant Manager & Team Lead at OnnoRokom EdTech Limited.

Today I lead Corporate Affairs, Brand Partnerships, Distribution (SCM), and School Communications for OnnoRokom BigganBaksho, a brand dedicated to helping children learn science by doing it. Holding a B.Sc. in Electrical & Electronic Engineering helps me seamlessly bridge the technical side of our products with strategic business growth.

  • Lead a cross-functional team of 50+ members, including a sales team of 15+ achieving 95% of annual targets.
  • Manage a network of 150+ vendors ensuring timely, cost-effective product distribution across Bangladesh.
  • Run nationwide school campaigns & "Biggan Utshob" festivals—bringing hands-on experiments to 4,000+ schools.
